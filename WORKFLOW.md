# Weekly workflow (for Claude)

Goal: a monetizable English YouTube Shorts channel of code-animated videos. One Short per day at
22:00 Asia/Tashkent, published through Metricool (brand blogId `7526089`, YouTube connected).

## Direction (from Ali, 2026-11-07)
- The whole channel must feel like ASMR: calm, smooth, satisfying pacing; soft pleasant sounds (gentle plucks, soft clicks, light chimes, airy whooshes), no harsh booms or loud beats; slow eases, no jarring cuts. Keep the music bed quiet and soft (low `vol`), let the satisfying sound effects lead.
- Ali found pure abstract/math animations (ball grows, sunflower angle, 1/2+1/4, cardioid, Monty Hall, color battle) too shallow or unclear. Do NOT make more of those.
- Make FUN-FACT videos from many sciences (space, biology, history, chemistry, geography, physics, human body…): one surprising, verified fact, told as a story — hook question → build-up → big reveal → "why"/bonus line → comment prompt. 20–33 s.
- Use `src/story.py` helpers (rich text highlights, progress bar, ding/whoosh/boom sounds). See `src/f1_earthday.py` … `src/f6_history.py` as reference quality.
- Every frame must be readable on a phone: big text, high contrast, one idea on screen at a time; content visible from the very first frame.

## Visual style: PAPER CUTOUT (from 2026-11-07, Ali's choice)
- All new videos use the paper-cutout look: `src/paper.py` (`paper()` torn-edge shapes with paper texture + layered drop shadows, `pin()` brass fasteners, jointed limbs) — see `src/p1_octopus.py` as the reference video.
- Stop-motion feel: animate on `ts = floor(t*12)/12`. Warm, textured palettes; text on torn paper strips (`banner()` in p1_octopus.py).
- ASMR sound: soft ambience, music-box plucks, gentle paper/bubble/heartbeat sounds — no booms.
- Story: question banner on frame 1 → reveal by ~3 s → 2–3 extra "and…" beats → short closing line ("NATURE IS WEIRD." / "follow for more paper facts").

## Text rules (from Ali, 2026-11-07)
- Much LESS text: max ~3–5 words per banner, at most 1–2 banners on screen, each readable for ≥2 s. Let the picture tell the story.
- Place text in the comfortable reading zone: top-centre, y ≈ 230–650 px. Never put important text in the bottom ~25% (YouTube title/buttons cover it) or near the right edge (like/comment buttons).

## Lessons from analytics
- "What π actually is" (first 6.5 h): 909 views, 92.8% from Shorts feed, avg view duration 0:16 of 0:18, but only 35.2% "stayed to watch" (64.8% swiped away). People who stay watch almost to the end; the FIRST 1–2 SECONDS lose most viewers.
- Hook rules: motion on frame 1 (no static intro, no fade-in); the question/hook text is on screen from frame 1; the main action starts within 0.5 s; keep 12–20 s; end in a way that loops back to the start.

## Rules (YouTube "inauthentic content" policy — keep the channel monetizable)
- Every video is a NEW idea/mechanic. Never re-upload a previous video with only colors/speed changed.
- Rotate formats: Satisfying (physics/math art, little or no text) · Educational (one clear idea, big English text) · Comparison (A vs B, sizes, speeds, numbers).
- Facts and numbers must be correct — check them on the web before rendering.
- Visuals and music are 100% generated by code (no stock footage, no copyrighted music, no real people, no brands/characters).
- `isAiGeneratedContent: false` (nothing realistic/synthetic), `madeForKids: false`.

## Style
- 1080x1920, 30 fps, 15–25 s, H.264 main + AAC, `-movflags +faststart`. Keep each file < 30 MB.
- Gen Z palette in `src/common.py` (`PAL`, `grad()`): #7B61FF #FF5CE1 #FF8A3D #FFE14D #B8FF3C #3CF0FF on deep purple-black.
- Font: "Inter Display Black" via `label()`. Hook text in the top 400 px, payoff/answer near the bottom.
- Music: `music()` synth bed + plucks on events (`pluck`, `note`, `Track`). Strong payoff moment near the end.
- Always render stills (`python3 x.py stills N…`) and look at them before the full render.

## Environment setup
`pip install --break-system-packages pycairo numpy pillow` (ffmpeg and the Inter fonts are usually present).

## Each week
1. `git pull`. Read `videos/*/week*.json` and the "Used ideas" list below — do not repeat.
2. Make 7 new videos → `videos/weekN/` (+ source in `src/wN_*.py`), and `videos/weekN/weekN.json`
   with: day, file, fmt, title (≤ 70 chars, ends with `#shorts`), desc (2 lines + 5 hashtags), tags (5–6), category
   (`EDUCATION` | `SCIENCE_TECHNOLOGY` | `ENTERTAINMENT`).
3. Commit + push to `main`. Check `https://raw.githubusercontent.com/alizairov/shorts-factory/main/videos/weekN/<file>` returns 200.
4. Find the last scheduled date with Metricool `getScheduledPosts`, then schedule the 7 new videos on the following
   7 days at 22:00 Asia/Tashkent with `createScheduledPost` (providers `[{"network":"youtube"}]`,
   `youtubeData.type = "short"`, `privacy = "public"`, media = the raw GitHub URL, `text` = desc).
5. Add the new ideas to "Used ideas" below and push.
