# A rainbow is a full circle — Impasto short (1080x1920, ~17 s)

Brief: Visual Brain short. Guess-first fact, shown by painting, no characters, minimal text, calm sound, logo + @Visual.Brainer.
Fact: a rainbow is a full circle of light (42° around the antisolar point); from the ground the horizon hides the lower part. From a plane or mountain top you can see the whole ring (NOAA/NCAR/Met Office).

Three structures
A. Tilt-up / crane-up: ground-level after-rain meadow with a half arc -> camera rises through cloud -> ground falls away -> arc completes into a ring around the plane's shadow.
B. Same wide repeated: day 1 half arc; a knife paint-over "lowers the horizon" and the arc continues.
C. Locked frame, light moves: the horizon line itself is scraped away by the knife, revealing the missing half underneath.

Choice: A (signature move = the crane-up), with C's scrape as the moment the ring closes.
Beats: 0-0.6 bare-canvas start (first knife strokes already landing: hook) -> 0.6-4 meadow + half rainbow laid in by the knife (appear sweep), title "HALF A CIRCLE?" laid in -> 4-9 crane-up: meadow slides down, clouds pass, horizon drops -> 9-12 the lower half of the arc is knifed in, ring closes, raking light peak -> 12-15 "IT'S A FULL CIRCLE." -> 15-17 scraped back toward the start plate for the loop.
Colour: cobalt -> peach after-rain sky, ochre/olive meadow; purest chroma reserved for the rainbow.
Sound: palette-knife scrapes on each repaint, soft rain-drip bed fading out, harp arpeggio rising as the ring closes, one glockenspiel when it completes.
